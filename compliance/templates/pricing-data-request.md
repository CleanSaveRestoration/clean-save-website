# Pricing source request (homeowner sends; we prepare)

Use when a carrier cuts rates or quantities "per price list" or software (rule WA-PRICING-SOURCE). The right to the source data belongs to the claimant, so the homeowner sends or signs this. Clean Save attaches its documentation.

---

Subject: Claim [CLAIM #], request for pricing data

Dear [ADJUSTER NAME],

Your estimate dated [DATE] reduced the mitigation charges from Clean Save Restoration by [AMOUNT].

Washington rules say a reasonable investigation may not rely solely on a database, estimating software, or benchmarks (WAC 284-30-330(4)). For each reduced line, please tell me the date, location, and suppliers of the data used for the material prices and labor rates (WAC 284-30-380(7)-(8)).

Attached is Clean Save's documentation for the work: daily moisture readings, equipment logs, and photos.

Thank you,
[INSURED NAME]
[PHONE / EMAIL]

---

Log it: set `claim.pricing_data_requested` to the date sent.
